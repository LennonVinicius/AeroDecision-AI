# ✈️ AeroDecision AI

### Intelligent Aerospace Decision System powered by AI, Data Analytics and Operational Intelligence

---

## 📖 About the Project

AeroDecision AI is an intelligent aerospace platform focused on assisting airlines, defense operations and aircraft manufacturers in choosing the best aircraft for a specific mission or flight route.

The project combines:

* Artificial Intelligence (LLMs)
* Data Analytics
* Operational Scoring
* Route Analysis
* Telemetry Processing
* Power BI Dashboards

to generate intelligent recommendations and strategic operational insights.

The goal is to simulate a real enterprise aviation decision-support environment using modern technologies and data-driven analysis.

# 🧠 How it Works

## Step 1

User describes the mission:

* Origin
* Destination
* Passengers
* Cargo
* Mission type
* Operational priority

Example:

```text id="gpmgh0"
Executive mission between São José dos Campos and Brasília
with 8 passengers prioritizing operational efficiency.
```

---

## Step 2

The system searches operational data:

* Airports
* Aircraft specifications
* Weather conditions
* Historical mission profiles

---

## Step 3

Python analytical engine calculates:

* Route distance
* Fuel estimation
* Aircraft compatibility
* Operational efficiency
* Estimated operational cost

---

## Step 4

The LLM receives mission context and generates:

* Intelligent recommendation
* Operational explanation
* Strategic insights

---

## Step 5

The scoring engine ranks aircraft based on:

* Cost
* Autonomy
* Efficiency
* Mission adequacy
* Operational constraints

---

## Step 6

Frontend displays:

* Recommended aircraft
* Aircraft ranking
* AI explanation
* Power BI dashboards

# 📊 Database Structure

## aircrafts

Stores aircraft technical specifications:

* range
* passengers
* speed
* operational cost
* fuel consumption

## airports

Stores airport operational information:

* ICAO/IATA
* runway data
* location coordinates

## missions

Stores mission requests and operational context.

## aircraft_scores

Stores analytical scoring results generated by the system.

## ai_recommendations

Stores AI-generated operational recommendations and explanations.
